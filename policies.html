Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2025

Good Care Children's Ministry ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website.

Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to operate our website effectively:

  • Contact Form Submissions: When you contact us through our website, we collect your name, email address, and message content to respond to your inquiry.
  • Donation Information: All donations are processed through Give Lively, a third-party payment processor. We do not collect or store your payment card information directly. Please review Give Lively's Privacy Policy for information about how they handle your data.

Cookies and Tracking

We do not use cookies or tracking technologies on this website. We do not use analytics services, advertising pixels, or any other tracking mechanisms. Your browsing activity on our site is not tracked or monitored.

How We Use Your Information

Information collected through our contact form is used solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiries
  • Provide information about our ministry
  • Send updates about our work (only if you request them)

Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties. We may share information only when required by law or to protect our rights.

Financial Transparency

Last updated: April 2025

Our Commitment

Good Care Children's Ministry is committed to the highest standards of financial transparency and accountability. We believe donors have the right to know how their contributions are used.

Tax-Exempt Status

Good Care Children's Ministry is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. Our Employer Identification Number (EIN) is 41-4766797. All donations are tax-deductible to the full extent permitted by law.

Donation Processing

All online donations are securely processed through Give Lively, a trusted nonprofit fundraising platform. Give Lively provides:

  • Secure, encrypted payment processing
  • Automatic tax receipts for your records
  • Full transaction tracking and transparency
  • Zero platform fees (100% of your donation reaches us, minus standard payment processing fees)

How Funds Are Used

Your donations directly support our mission to care for orphans and widows in Uganda, including:

  • Food, shelter, and basic necessities for children in our care
  • Educational expenses including school fees, uniforms, and supplies
  • Healthcare and medical needs
  • Spiritual development and Christian education programs
  • Agricultural initiatives through our "Farming God's Way" program
  • Support services for widows and single mothers

Accountability

We maintain accurate financial records and are committed to responsible stewardship of all donations received. For questions about our finances or to request additional information, please contact us at rufus.forrest@goodcareministry.com.

Donation Receipts

Tax receipts are automatically generated and emailed to you by Give Lively immediately after your donation is processed. If you need a duplicate receipt or have questions about your donation, please contact us.

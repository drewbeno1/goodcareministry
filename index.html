About Good Care Children's Ministry

Serving with love since 2012

Our sole purpose is to support orphans, vulnerable children at risk, and aid widowed or single mothers struggling to provide essential care.

Good Care started in 2012 as a christian based organization in response to the present ugandan needs around the founders. Now, led by Timothy Sserugga, Good Care Children's Ministry is still going strong, providing shelter, food, education, and spritual guidance for more than 100 children whose ages range from 18 months to 21 years.
